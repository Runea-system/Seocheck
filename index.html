Questions

Before you connect your site.

The short answers. Anything else, reach out and a human will reply.

How does SeoChecker make changes to my site?＋

It opens a pull request with the exact diff, a plain-language explanation, and a link back to what changed. Nothing merges without your review unless you turn on auto-merge.

What does the weekly crawl actually look at?＋

Titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, canonical tags, sharing images, and a handful of other technical signals that quietly break as a site grows.

Is the free analyzer the same engine as the product?＋

It runs a smaller, deterministic slice of the same checks, entirely in your browser. Nothing you paste is uploaded or stored.

Do I need to install anything?＋

No. Connect a repository or a sitemap, and the weekly crawl starts on its own.
